Exercise 1

For this exercise, we were tasked with making a face using if and for loop statments I decided to make a cat for my face. I have 3 cats of my own so I decided to use them as reference. Since I am new to coding and its a bit harder for me, I made a very simiple cat face. I made a simple face with 2 eyes, a nose and ears. Originally I wanted to do a W shaped mouth for the cat however I wasn't sure how to do that and change the location without making it difficult. I wanted to make it look more animated so I added a cat toy that follows the mouse to the left and right. I also wanted to make the eyes move to follow the ball/mouse however I was having trouble tracking it and keeping it inside the eye socket(ellpise). For the background I used the example code and changed the colors and also the shapes of the "lights".

Project 1

For the brushes I made most of them can create detail which makes it easier to work with when drawing. 2 of the brushes I have are easier for the background and just abstract parts that help fill the canvas. I also wanted to make sure that the colors matched and chose a cool color scheme. For the various drawings I choose to draw something things im interested in and I also recreated one of my old drawings.
